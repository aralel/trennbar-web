Two thirds of the dictionary carries no suffix at all — those entries are verbs built from a prefix and a stem. The suffixes that do appear are mostly derivational endings that turn a stem into a noun or an adjective (-ismus, -ist, -schaft, -keit), so they cluster on the nouns rather than the separable verbs.
