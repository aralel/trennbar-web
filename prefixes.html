Separable verb prefixes (ab, aus, ein, ver) dominate the head of this list; the lowercase ones are the prefixes the app's first cylinder turns. The capitalised entries (Haus, Welt, Volks) are noun compounds, which is why they sit in the long tail — each contributes only a handful of words.
